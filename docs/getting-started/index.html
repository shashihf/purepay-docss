    Set up the app

    Install PurePay

    Download PurePay from the App Store or Google Play.

    Create your profile

    Open the app and enter your details. Your profile is what other people see when you pay them.

    Back up your account

    Write down your recovery details and keep them somewhere safe. You need them to restore your account on a new phone.

    Wait for approval

    New profiles are approved by an existing member before you can make payments.

    Warning

    Never share your recovery details with anyone, including the PurePay team.

    Next steps

    Once your profile is approved, follow the Quickstart to make your first payment.
